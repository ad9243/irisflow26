A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Do I need to know anything about AI?+

No. We’ll start with how your business works and explain the options in plain language. You bring the context; we bring the technical expertise.

Can you work with the tools I already use?+

Often, yes. We review the integrations and API access your existing tools support before proposing a solution. We’ll be clear about any limitations.

How long does a project take?+

It depends on the scope and complexity. After our discovery conversation, we’ll share a realistic timeline and clear milestones before work begins.

What about privacy and human oversight?+

We discuss data access, permissions, and approval steps during discovery. Sensitive workflows should have clear boundaries and a person available to make important decisions.
